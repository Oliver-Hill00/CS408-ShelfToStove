Shopping List

Coming in Checkpoint 5

How it works

1
Stock your pantry. Enter what you have, with amounts and expiration dates.
2
Ask "What can I cook?" Recipes are ranked by how much of each ingredient you have on hand.
3
Fill the gaps. Send just the shortfall to your shopping list in one click.
